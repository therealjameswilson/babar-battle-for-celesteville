# Arthur’s Motorbike — v0.53.0

Recruit at Artillery Works: 180 Supplies, 60 Materials, 20 seconds, one population.
Only one Arthur may be alive or queued. He has 180 health and 155 base movement
speed, benefits from the existing council movement bonus, and has no standard
weapon. Losing him allows paid recruitment again. His existing council power and
crossover relationship entry remain intact.

Completing the faction’s first atomic or H-bomb payload permanently unlocks his
neutron strike for that match; research alone does not. Select Arthur, choose
“Arthur: neutron strike,” then tap a visible target within 360m. Launch costs
400 Supplies, 120 Materials and 30 Uranium, with a 120-second cooldown. Invalid
targets spend nothing. Only one friendly nuclear strike may be in flight at once.

The fixed target receives a 12-second public warning and a 110m blast. Base damage
is 450 to mobile units and 80 to buildings, reduced by distance and existing combat
rules. Friendly fire applies. Arthur can move during the countdown; his destruction
aborts it without refund. His motorbike does not require a building supply link.
Civil Defense warning evacuation and worker protection apply normally.

All weapon behavior and numbers are fictional game mechanics, not real weapon
specifications or a claim of a harmless blast. The smaller cloud is an illustrative
variant of the existing local artwork. Vehicle rendering combines original Canvas
wheels/frame/lamps with Arthur’s existing local portrait crop. His motorbike weapon
role is invented for this game, not attributed to the books or television series.
