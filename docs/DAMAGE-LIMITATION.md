# Austin Long: Damage Limitation — v0.52.0

Research at an idle Guard School: **150 Supplies, 50 Materials, 40 seconds**.
Completion unlocks Civil Defense construction for that faction. Shelters retain their
separate 180 Supplies / 80 Materials building cost and existing evacuation behavior.
No nuclear weapons research is required, allowing investment in protection first.

An unfinished, cancelled or destroyed research project does not unlock the building.
The usual 75% cancellation refund and isolated research speed apply. Rhino command
uses the same paid research and construction gate, considering the skill after six
minutes. Restart clears both factions' unlocks.

## Attribution

Inspired by Austin Long's essay **“Strategic Nuclear Targeting Policy: Necessity and
Damage Limitation”** in the H-Diplo/ISSF *Policy Roundtable 1-4 on U.S. Nuclear Policy*
(22 December 2016):
https://issforum.org/policy/1-4-nuclear

Long discusses damage limitation in the broader context of nuclear strategy and
extended deterrence. The Civil Defense unlock is an original, deliberately narrow
game adaptation. The research price, timing, shelter behavior and protection
percentage are invented balance rules, not claims from his work. This attribution
does not add him to Babar's fictional family roster or imply his endorsement.

As of v0.56.0, the same completed skill also unlocks Missile Defense Batteries.
Their construction and interceptor shots have separate costs; see MISSILES.md.
