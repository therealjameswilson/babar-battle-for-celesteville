# Leader nuclear authority — v0.54.0

Babar authorizes elephant nuclear launches; Rataxes authorizes rhino launches.
A living faction leader must personally have direct sight to a completed friendly
Royal Palace / Fortress (core) or Field Headquarters. The headquarters must be
within the leader's own vision radius, with no forest rectangle or other building
blocking the segment to its footprint. Allied scouts, global reveal powers and
supply links do not replace this requirement. Enemy headquarters never qualify.

Atomic and H-bomb assembly still happens at Artillery Works. Ready factories now
instruct the player to select Babar. Launch controls appear on the selected leader.
Arthur still carries the neutron payload, but Babar's actions contain “Authorize
neutron strike”; Arthur cannot initiate targeting himself. Existing costs, payload
acquisition, range, cooldown, visibility and supply requirements remain in force.

Authority is checked both when opening targeting and when committing the target.
A failed check consumes no payload or resources. Authorization is a launch-time
requirement: subsequent movement of the leader does not recall an authorized
strike. Destroying/isolation of the launcher retains the existing abort rules.
The rhino AI uses the same authority check; this change does not introduce a new
faction-selection screen to the existing elephant-player skirmish.
