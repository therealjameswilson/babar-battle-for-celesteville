Description draft:

Command your army, build a working supply line, and defend Celesteville in a
single-player real-time strategy skirmish. Gather supplies, materials and uranium;
construct defenses; recruit infantry, scouts and field guns; and coordinate the
family council. Story and Commander difficulties offer different levels of
pressure. Research advanced weapons and missile defense, or win through combined
arms. Touch orders, local checkpoints and offline play are included. No account,
advertising or in-app purchases.

## Review notes draft

The game and every playable asset are bundled. No sign-in or network connection
is needed. Tap Start on the briefing. Use the field manual for touch controls.
Opening the character archive pauses the battle; dismiss it and resume. Use the
pause panel to save and continue a local checkpoint. The app supports portrait
and both landscape orientations. Audio starts after player interaction.

The game contains stylized military combat, firearms, flamethrowers and fictional
nuclear weapons; no graphic gore. Complete Apple's actual age-rating questionnaire
accurately. Do not list in the Kids category or claim an official Babar license
without authorization. Add evidence of third-party content authorization to the
review submission when available, including applicable imagery/photographs.

## Privacy and encryption draft

No developer data collection/tracking/analytics/ads or user accounts are present
in the current native source. Battle saves/preferences remain on-device in
WebKit storage. External support links open the destination site and follow its
policies. The app uses Apple platform networking only for user-chosen links and
has no custom cryptography. Confirm these answers in App Store Connect for the
final binary rather than treating this document as a submitted declaration.
