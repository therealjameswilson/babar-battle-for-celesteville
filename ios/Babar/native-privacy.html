Privacy policy

Native iPhone app · Updated October 4, 2026

The game does not collect or transmit personal information to its developer. It has no accounts, advertisements, analytics, purchases, or tracking SDKs. The complete game is bundled in the app and does not need a network connection.

On-device storage

Your battle checkpoints, sound settings, and preferences are stored locally using WebKit website storage. They are not sent to a game server. Deleting the app removes its local data. Apple device backups may include app data according to your device settings.

External links

If you choose a source or support link, it opens outside the game. The destination service, including GitHub, handles that visit under its own privacy policy. Apple's operating-system diagnostics are governed by Apple and your device settings.

Support

You can contact the project through GitHub Issues. Issues are public: do not post personal information, passwords, or secrets. Information you voluntarily submit there is handled by GitHub.
